FD Dots is a modified version of Doto by Oliver Lalan (https://github.com/oliverlalan/Doto),
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).

Changes: one fixed weight with round dots, two-dot colon, one-dot full stop, rounded-box zero, narrower colon and space.
build-fd-dots.py makes it from the original font.
